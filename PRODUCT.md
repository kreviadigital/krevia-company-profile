## Users

- Primary users: Construction business owners, project managers, operations directors, and commercial decision-makers seeking to connect project operations, site progress, budgets, and procurement in one cohesive system.
- Secondary users: Business owners and operational leaders in project-based or asset-heavy industries evaluating integrated ERP systems to replace fragmented spreadsheets and manual messaging.

## Product Purpose

Krevia Digital builds integrated ERP software products to help businesses connect operational workflows, improve visibility, and make data-driven decisions. The initial flagship product direction is **Krevia Construction ERP**—an integrated system designed to manage project operations, RAB/budgeting, site progress, cost control, procurement, inventory, and workforce management.

Success means presenting a credible, trustworthy, and realistic ERP product company that attracts qualified business prospects, introduces Krevia's ERP product roadmap with technical integrity, and drives high-intent discovery inquiries.

## Positioning

"Krevia Digital builds integrated ERP solutions that help businesses connect operations, improve visibility, and make better decisions."

- Tagline: *Build Smarter. Operate Better.*
- Mechanism: Unlike conventional monolithic legacy ERPs or generic digital agencies offering surface-level websites, Krevia focuses on bespoke, modular ERP systems engineered around practical, field-tested operational workflows without vendor lock-in or inflated marketing buzzwords.
- Maturity posture: Proudly early-stage and honest. Construction ERP is introduced as an active product in development / early access, explicitly differentiating between currently available capabilities, modules in development, and future roadmap items.

## Product Principles

1. **Practical Utility Over Marketing Slop**: Ground every capability in actual operational problems (spreadsheets chaos, budget variances, site reconciliation) rather than vanity buzzwords.
2. **Honesty on Maturity**: State clearly what is built, what is under active development, and what is planned. High-craft engineering earns trust through transparency.
3. **Structured Clarity**: Information architecture must enable a business owner or project manager to evaluate product relevance in seconds.
4. **Resilient Engineering**: Fast load times, zero bloatware, full accessibility, and flawless mobile-to-desktop responsiveness.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliant contrast ratios across dark navy and light surfaces.
- Accessible keyboard navigation and ARIA attributes for all interactive elements (menus, tabs, forms).
- Full support for `prefers-reduced-motion` across all micro-interactions.
